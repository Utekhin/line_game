Human vs AI

Human vs Human

Play against a friend online. Create a game and share the link to invite them.

Human vs Human

Play against a friend online. Build fences from border to border to capture pieces!
Rules: Complete a fence (border-to-border chain) to capture enclosed opponent pieces. Win by exhausting opponent's pool of 50 pieces!
